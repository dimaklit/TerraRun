TerraRun Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TerraRun (dimaklit.github.io/TerraRun) is a territory-control game for runners. It turns GPS routes that you record or import into claimed territory on a map. This policy describes what data TerraRun collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account data. Your email address and a hashed password, used only to sign you in.
  • Activity data. GPS routes you import (GPX/GeoJSON files) or sync from connected services, including coordinates, timestamps, distance and the territory computed from them.
  • Connected services. If you link a Garmin account, we store OAuth access tokens and your Garmin user ID so that your new activities can be synced automatically. Tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the browser.

We do not collect health metrics (heart rate, sleep, body data), do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

How your data is used

Activity data is used solely to display your routes and territories to you inside TerraRun. Your routes and territories are private to your account. Data from connected services is processed only to convert activities into routes for the game.

Where your data is stored

  • Neon (neon.tech) — database hosting (AWS, EU region) for accounts, routes and tokens.
  • Cloudflare Workers — serverless backend that handles the Garmin connection and activity sync.
  • GitHub Pages — static hosting for the application itself; it stores no user data.

Garmin data

When you connect Garmin, TerraRun receives only your activity files (GPS track and timestamps) through the Garmin Connect Developer Program. You can disconnect Garmin at any time from the account menu, which deletes the stored tokens. Disconnecting the TerraRun app in your Garmin account settings has the same effect.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us and we will remove it.

Contact

TerraRun is operated by Dmitry Kliteynik. For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/dimaklit/TerraRun/issues or contact @dimaklit on GitHub.
